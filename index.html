OPC UA · Modbus TCP · Digital Twins

Bring Tinkercad Circuits into the industrial world.

Tinkercad Industrial Bridge connects Arduino circuits simulated in your browser with automation and digital-twin tools through standard industrial protocols.

Explore sample projects See the demo
What it does

Test industrial integrations with a browser-based circuit.

Use a simulated Arduino as a source of live tags for automation software, training exercises, and digital-twin experiments.

OPC UA

Expose circuit variables

Map serial values to OPC UA nodes for monitoring and control from compatible clients.

MODBUS TCP

Connect to Modbus clients

Map circuit tags to Modbus coils and registers for industrial simulations.

LOCAL SOFTWARE

Keep the bridge on your machine

A browser extension works with a local Node.js bridge to relay circuit data.
